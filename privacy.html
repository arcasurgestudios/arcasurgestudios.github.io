Privacy Policy for Chip Stack Sort

Effective Date: November 9, 2026
Developer: Arca Surge Studios

Arca Surge Studios ("we", "us", or "our") built the Chip Stack Sort mobile game as a free, ad-supported application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play our game.

1. Information We Collect Directly

Our game does not require user registration or account creation. We do not directly collect, store, or request any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise GPS location.

2. Game Progress & Local Storage

Your gameplay progress (such as current level, coins, and daily login streaks) is saved locally on your device. If enabled in your Android settings, Android Auto Backup may back up your local game preferences to your personal Google Drive account so your progress can be restored if you reinstall the game.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements. Google AdMob may automatically collect and share certain device-level information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention purposes, including:

  • Device and Other IDs: Android Advertising ID (AAID) and App Set ID.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse geographic location inferred from your device's IP address.
  • App Interactions: App launches, gameplay session activity, and ad views/clicks.
  • Diagnostics & Crash Logs: Device specifications, performance metrics, and crash data.

All data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Users can reset or delete their Advertising ID at any time inside their Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Learn more about how Google processes data at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because our app does not create user accounts or store personal profiles on our own servers, we do not hold personal account records to delete. Local game progress can be erased at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android device settings or uninstalling the app, and Advertising ID associations can be deleted in your Android Google Ads settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Our game is intended for a general audience (ages 13+) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
